About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineer with expertise in Computational Mechanics, Artificial Intelligence, Data-Driven Engineering, Finite Element Analysis, Bayesian Inference, Inverse Problems, Structural Health Monitoring, Machine Learning, and Digital Twin technologies. I obtained my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, where my research focused on integrating advanced finite element methods with data-driven techniques for solving inverse engineering problems. Currently, I serve as Senior Technical Lead (Simulation) at IMECONSYS Private Limited, Puducherry, leading simulation-driven product development for Industrial IoT applications.
