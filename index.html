About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineer specializing in Computational Mechanics, Finite Element Analysis, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Structural Health Monitoring, Bayesian Inference, Digital Engineering, and Product Design. My research integrates physics-based numerical methods with data-driven artificial intelligence techniques to solve challenging engineering problems involving inverse analysis, uncertainty quantification and digital twins.
